### Steps
Use your best judgement for best order of operation, as this will help in the "ease" of assembly --> List order is not necassarily the best order of operations.  Flux and Solder paste is your friend in this build.
1. Solder R1, R2, C5, U5 (looks like J5), C6, C7
2. Solder R6, D2, C1, C2, C3, C4, R3, R4
3. Solder U3
4. Solder U2
5. Solder U1
6. Found that solder paste is the easiest to place and solder J9

7. Cut the wires to approximately 35MM and strip the ends to approximately 5MM and tin the wire tips
8. Solder the wires to the expansion boards, creating a chain
<img src="https://github.com/cyberm3n-org/SC_2026/blob/main/ref_images/fd_chain.png" width="25%">

9. Solder the chain of expansion boards to the main board
10. Solder the Header PINs to each of the boards
11. Good time to test the build and verify that everything is soldered correctly

12. Place the boards into the top/open housing parts (they are directional)
13. Snap the bottom and top housing parts together
14. [OPTIONAL] [RECOMMENDED] Place Hot Glue just inside the housing unit holding the wires inplace.  Be cautious to not place to much glue, as it will need to be trimmed to ensure that the housing units will fit snuggly into the display body
15. Place the Main board in the top position of the display body and work your way down the chain
16. [OPTIONAL] Place super glue into a couple of the corners
17. Add minibadges and enjoy
