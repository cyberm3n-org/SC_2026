### Steps
Use your best judgement for best order of operation, as this will help in the "ease" of assembly --> List order is not necassarily the best order of operations
*Recommend* Starting with the back of the board
1. Solder the Counter (U2) into place
2. Solder LEDs
3. Solder R3 Resistor
4. Solder the Header PINs into place (helps hold the PCB stable)
Now Solder the front components
5. Solder the Timer (U1) into place
6. Solder the Remaining Resistors
7. Solder the Capacitors into place
3. [OPTIONAL] Apply hot glue to the back side to difuse light
